# 3 Inventors Portal - Subscription Pricing Strategy

## Executive Summary

Following the expansion of the Portal feature set, the original pricing model of €348/year is no longer appropriate.

The platform has evolved from a business administration tool into a comprehensive business management platform with automation, financial intelligence, customer self-service capabilities, and integration support.

## Recommended Pricing

| Plan | Monthly Equivalent | Annual Charge |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Starter | €39 | €390 |
| Professional | €79 | €790 |
| Enterprise | €149 | €1490 |

## Positioning

- Starter = Foundation
- Professional = Automation
- Enterprise = Scale

## Key Conclusions

- Do not compete on being the cheapest.
- Compete on professionalism, reliability, and automation.
- Professional contains the highest value and should be priced accordingly.
- Present annual pricing as:
  - €39/month equivalent
  - Charged annually (€390/year)
- Maintain a 14-day Professional trial to demonstrate automation value.

## Final Recommendation

Launch with:

Starter: €390/year
Professional: €790/year
Enterprise: €1490/year

This pricing reflects the platform's current value, supports sustainable support costs, and positions the Portal as professional business software rather than a low-cost invoicing tool.
