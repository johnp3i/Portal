# 3 Inventors — Operational Proposal & Quotation System
## Agent Handoff / Proposal Design Specification

Version: 1.0  
Purpose: Allow any future AI agent or developer to consistently generate executive-grade 3 Inventors operational proposals and quotations.

---

3 Inventors proposals are NOT generic SaaS quotations.

The proposal system must reflect:

- operational seriousness
- structured execution
- operational visibility
- long-term reliability
- intelligence readiness
- operational partnership

The proposal must feel:

> Executive-grade operational infrastructure proposal

NOT:

> Startup SaaS pricing page

# 8. Proposal Structure

## Section Order

The structure should remain consistent.

---

## 1. Hero Section

Includes:

- proposal type
- logos
- client name
- operational summary
- prepared by section
- reference number
- proposal date
- valid until date

---

## 2. Operational Positioning

Purpose:
Explain the operational value.

Avoid feature lists.

Explain:

- operational structure
- visibility
- reliability
- operational continuity

---

## 3. Included Platforms

Short operational descriptions for:

- MyChair
- WorkforcePi
- Operational Awareness
- Labeling System
- ERP
- OPS
- JDS

Depending on proposal scope.

---

## 4. Commercial Proposal Table

Must include:

| Service | Monthly | Daily | Annual |

Daily operational cost is REQUIRED.

Reason:

It reframes the pricing psychologically into:

> Low daily operational cost

instead of:

> Large subscription amount

---

## 5. Total Summary Card

Must include:

- Total Monthly Subscription
- Total Daily Operational Cost
- Total Annual Subscription

Rules:

- All amounts same visual size
- Do not oversize totals
- Keep executive proportionality

---

## 6. Support & Continuity Section

VERY IMPORTANT.

This section explains:

- continuous support
- operational assistance
- continuous updates
- long-term platform evolution
- operational partnership

This section justifies premium subscription positioning.

The client must understand:

> They are not buying static software.

They are entering:

> Continuous operational platform relationship.

---

## 7. Commercial Notes

Includes:

- VAT excluded
- annual billing
- hardware exclusion
- scalability notes
- additional branch/user notes

---

## 8. Hardware Recommendation Section

Always recommend:

> Separate hardware proposal

Reason:

- cleaner operational pricing
- easier upgrades
- deployment flexibility
- cleaner commercial separation

---

## 9. Strategic Direction Footer

# 9. Pricing Psychology Rules

## Critical Principle

DO NOT sell features.

Sell:

- operational clarity
- visibility
- continuity
- support
- reliability
- operational structure

---

## Daily Cost Framing

Always calculate:

Monthly / 30

Examples:

- €79/month → €2.63/day
- €22/month → €0.73/day

This dramatically improves pricing perception.

---

## Premium Positioning

Never apologize for pricing.

Instead communicate:

- reliability
- continuity
- support
- operational partnership
- continuous updates
