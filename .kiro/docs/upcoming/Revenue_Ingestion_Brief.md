# Revenue Ingestion — Feature Brief

**Date:** 15 July 2026  
**Status:** Concept — Planning  
**Module:** Revenue / External Sales  
**Tier Placement:** Professional (basic) + Enterprise (full)

---

## Vision

Revenue Ingestion enables businesses to import external sales data (POS systems, Z-reports, third-party platforms) into Portal for unified financial visibility. This bridges the gap between Portal-issued invoices and revenue generated through other channels.
