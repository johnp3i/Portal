#### "Can I switch plans later?"
```
Yes. You can upgrade from Foundation to Professional at any time — your data stays exactly where it is. If you need to downgrade, automation features become inactive but all your records, invoices, and history remain accessible.
```

```
Your data belongs to you. If you cancel, your account is deactivated but your data is preserved for 90 days. You can export invoices, customer records, and purchase history as CSV at any time before or after cancellation.
```

```
We offer annual billing only. A business should never lose access to its operational platform because of a missed monthly payment. Annual billing means one payment, twelve months of uninterrupted access — and you save two months compared to monthly pricing.
```

```
Yes. The platform is fully responsive. You can view your dashboard, check outstanding invoices, record payments, and manage customers from any mobile browser. No app download required.
```

## Tone & Copy Rules for the Website Agent

1. **No superlatives** — never "best", "amazing", "revolutionary"
2. **No hype** — never "game-changing", "cutting-edge", "next-generation"
3. **Outcome over feature** — "the platform chases payments" not "automated payment reminder system"
4. **Short sentences** — max 20 words per sentence where possible
5. **Active voice** — "Create quotations" not "Quotations can be created"
6. **Concrete over abstract** — "€1,600 recovered" not "significant revenue improvement"
7. **Calm confidence** — the platform speaks for itself, no exclamation marks
8. **Respect the visitor** — no urgency tactics, no countdown timers, no "limited time" pressure
