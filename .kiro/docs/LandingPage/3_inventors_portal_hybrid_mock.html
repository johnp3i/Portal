Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about how the platform works, what's included, and how to get started.

What modules are included in the plan?

Everything currently available: quotations, invoicing, credit notes, revenue control, customer registry, product catalogue, VAT submissions, purchases, supplier management, customer statements, audit logs, and CSV/PDF exports.

How many users can I add?

The current plan supports up to 5 users. Each user gets their own login with configurable module access (full or read-only per module). Need more users? Contact us for a tailored setup.

Is my data isolated from other businesses?

Yes. The platform enforces strict tenant isolation — each business only sees their own customers, invoices, purchases, and financial data. No cross-tenant data leakage is possible.

Can I convert quotations to invoices?

Yes. Quotations follow a lifecycle (Draft → Sent → Accepted → Converted) and can be converted to invoices with a single action. The invoice captures an immutable snapshot of the quotation at the time of conversion.

How does VAT submission work?

The platform automatically generates VAT periods based on your registration date. It computes output VAT from issued invoices and input VAT from purchases, calculates the net payable, and produces a full period report with monthly breakdowns.

Can I import existing purchase data?

Yes. The platform supports CSV import for purchases with a preview-and-confirm workflow. You can also create suppliers and expense categories inline during bulk entry.

What payment methods do you support for the subscription?

Subscription payments are handled securely through Stripe. You can pay with any major credit or debit card. The plan is billed annually at €348/year.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. You can start a free trial to explore the platform before committing. No credit card required during the trial period.
