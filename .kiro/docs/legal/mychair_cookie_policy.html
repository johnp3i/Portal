Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how cookies and similar technologies may be used in connection with MyChair-related web interfaces.

1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small text files or similar storage mechanisms placed on a browser or device in order to support functionality, security, session management, analytics, preferences, or related purposes.

2. Types of Cookies We May Use

  • Essential cookies: required for basic platform operation, authentication, session continuity, and security.
  • Preference cookies: used to remember interface settings or user choices where applicable.
  • Performance or analytics cookies: used to understand usage patterns and improve platform quality, where enabled.
  • Security-related storage: used to support secure login, fraud reduction, and controlled session handling.

3. Why Cookies Are Used

Cookies may be used to keep users signed in, protect sessions, remember preferences, improve usability, measure service performance, and support secure access to platform features.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Where third-party tools or integrations are used, they may place their own cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies. This depends on the selected site configuration and enabled services.

5. Managing Cookie Preferences

Users can usually control cookies through browser settings, consent tools, or device controls. Blocking some cookies may reduce site functionality or prevent login/session workflows from working as intended.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated to reflect changes in website functionality, legal requirements, or technology choices.
