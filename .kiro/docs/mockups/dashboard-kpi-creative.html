Creative KPI Alternatives

Beyond cards — different ways to present the same 8 data points with minimal vertical footprint.

Option E — Natural Language Summary

KPIs presented as a readable sentence. Humans parse prose faster than scanning grid cells. One line, all context. Zero cards.

This month you've received €12,450.00 in revenue (8 payments), with €18,320.50 outstanding across 12 invoices and €4,780.00 overdue. Expenses total €6,230.00 (14 purchases). Quotations: 3 drafts, 7 sent (€24,850 pipeline), 4 accepted (57%), 21 active customers.
