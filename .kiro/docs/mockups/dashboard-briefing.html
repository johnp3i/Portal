Dashboard Briefing — Design Options

Three visual approaches for the narrative summary card. Same data, different presentation.

Option A: Flowing Paragraph

Clean, minimal. Reads like a written brief. Compact vertical space. Links are inline.

Good morning. You have 3 overdue invoices totalling €4,200.00 — the oldest is 12 days past due (Le Paris Roasting). View receivables →  ·  2 proposals worth €8,500.00 are awaiting acceptance. Follow up →  ·  5 purchases are not yet assigned to a VAT period. Assign now →  ·  Cash flow looks healthy for the next 30 days.

Option B: Structured Lines

Each insight on its own line. Easier to scan quickly. Still narrative tone. Links at end of each line.

Good morning — here's your briefing.
You have 3 overdue invoices totalling €4,200.00 — the oldest is 12 days past due (Le Paris Roasting). View →
2 proposals worth €8,500.00 are awaiting client acceptance. Follow up →
5 purchases are not yet assigned to a VAT period. Assign →
Cash flow looks healthy for the next 30 days.
A payment of €1,200.00 was received from Acme Wholesale yesterday.

Option C: Card with Icon Rows

More visual. Uses coloured icons to signal urgency (red = overdue, amber = needs action, green = good). Feels more like a notification centre.

Good morning
Your operational briefing for today
3 overdue invoices totalling €4,200.00 — oldest is 12 days past due (Le Paris Roasting).
View →
2 proposals worth €8,500.00 are awaiting acceptance.
Follow up →
5 purchases not yet assigned to a VAT period.
Assign →
Cash flow looks healthy for the next 30 days.
A payment of €1,200.00 was received from Acme Wholesale yesterday.

Option D: All Clear State

What it looks like when nothing needs attention.

Good afternoon. Everything looks good — no items need your attention right now. A payment of €1,200.00 was received from Acme Wholesale this morning.
