Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch plans at any time? ▾
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately. When upgrading, you'll be charged a prorated amount for the remainder of your billing cycle. When downgrading, the new rate applies at the start of your next billing period.
What happens to my data if I downgrade? ▾
All your data is preserved. Professional features become read-only — you can still view historical reports, forecasts, and audit logs, but new automated actions (reminders, schedules) will be paused until you upgrade again.
Is there a free trial? ▾
Yes. Every new account starts with a 14-day Professional trial with full access to all features. No credit card required. At the end of the trial, you can choose Foundation or Professional.
Do I need a credit card to start? ▾
No. You can start with Foundation immediately without entering payment details. A credit card is only required when you choose to subscribe to a paid plan or when your trial ends and you want to continue with Professional.
