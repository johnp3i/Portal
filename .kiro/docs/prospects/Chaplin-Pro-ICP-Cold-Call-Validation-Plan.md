# Chaplin Pro — Founder-Led ICP Validation & Cold-Call Plan

**Purpose:** Move from opportunistic leads to a controlled, measurable founder-led market-validation campaign for Chaplin Pro.

## 1. Working ICP

Target organisations that:
1. Manage multiple concurrent projects, events, cases or client engagements.
2. Work extensively with external people: clients, speakers, sponsors, suppliers, parents, candidates, members, etc.
3. Repeatedly collect information, files, requirements, decisions or approvals.
4. Have operational work fragmented across email, WhatsApp, spreadsheets, shared drives, documents and calls.
5. Have several employees collaborating around the same project or external relationship.

> **Working ICP:** Service organisations managing repeatable projects that require substantial coordination between an internal team and external participants.

A prospect matching 4–5 characteristics is particularly interesting. Company size alone should not determine priority.

## 2. Initial Market Segments

### Priority 1 — Professional Event & Conference Organisations
Strongest immediate vertical because Chaplin Pro maps naturally to event workflows and we have early evidence from IMH.

Initial examples: EasyConferences, CPC Events, Event Makers and other professional conference/event organisers.

### Priority 2 — Experiential, PR, Advertising & Creative Agencies
Potential fit because of briefs, files, client decisions, approvals and multi-person project delivery.

Initial examples: Brand Connect, Magnify Communications, Purpose Communications, Ledra Advertising and AdBoards.

### Priority 3 — Independent Event Professionals / Planners
Smaller organisations may provide faster adoption and shorter decision cycles.

Initial examples: Splendid Events, Evgenia Panagiotou Event Planning and CorporateEventsCy.

### Additional Segments to Test
Venues/hotels; creative/video production; architects/interior designers; recruitment/HR; training organisations; academies/education; professional/project services.

Do not target every segment simultaneously. Discover where Chaplin Pro creates the strongest repeated demand.

### Lead Scoring
Score every prospect before calling:
- **ICP Fit:** /5
- **Pain Probability:** /5
- **Accessibility:** /5
- **Learning Value:** /5
- **Maximum:** 20

Priority: **16–20 = A**, **12–15 = B**, below 12 = hold/lower priority.

Prestige or size should not artificially increase the score.

## 4. Weekly Operating Rhythm

### Monday — Research & Qualification
Review pipeline; research and qualify prospects; identify appropriate public business contact/role; score leads; prepare one relevant hypothesis for each Priority-A lead.

### Tuesday–Thursday — Founder-Led Outreach
**Initial objective: 5 new targeted calls per day = 15 new cold calls/week**, plus follow-ups, demonstrations and active-pilot conversations.

Quality matters more than volume. These are discovery calls, not a call-centre campaign.

### Friday — Evidence Review & Next-Week Lead Generation
Review calls, meaningful conversations, emails requested, decision-makers reached, demos booked/completed, pilots proposed/active, conversions, objections, repeated pain points, missing capabilities and exact customer language.

Then prepare and qualify the **next week's lead list** based on the evidence.

## 5. First-Cohort Objectives

Treat these as validation objectives, not forecasts:
- 30 qualified organisations researched
- 30 attempted contacts
- 10+ meaningful conversations
- 5+ demonstrations
- 2+ real operational pilots
- 1+ paid conversion

If results fall short, identify where the funnel failed: ICP, contact, opening, pain severity, demo fit, product gap, pricing or adoption friction.

> **Primary learning objective:** Identify the 2–3 operational pains that repeatedly cause prospects to recognise themselves in Chaplin Pro.

## 6. Cold-Call Principle

Do not lead with features.

Avoid: “Chaplin Pro has surveys, decision boards, files, tasks…”

Prefer:

> **“We have developed a platform for organisations that manage projects involving a lot of collaboration with clients and external people. I'm trying to understand whether it addresses any of the operational work your team currently handles across email, messages, files and separate systems.”**

Then explore their workflow.

**Funnel:** Qualified lead → Cold call → Discovery → Relevant demonstration → Real workflow pilot → Paid adoption.

## 7. Evidence to Capture After Every Contact

Classify: No contact / Gatekeeper / Not relevant / Email requested / Decision-maker conversation / Demo booked / Demo completed / Pilot proposed / Pilot active / Paid customer.

Record:
1. Current tools/process.
2. Biggest operational friction.
3. Exact words used to describe the problem.
4. Chaplin capability generating strongest reaction.
5. Important capability expected but missing.
6. Support/software-provider complaints.
7. Willingness to pilot.
8. Willingness to pay.

**Do not measure success by “they liked the demo.” Measure movement toward a real operational workflow.**

## 8. Weekly Decision Rule

Every Friday ask:

> **What did this week's evidence teach us about who needs Chaplin Pro most?**

Use that answer to determine the following week's prospects. Increase exposure to segments showing strong signals and reduce exposure to weak ones.

> **Evidence matters more than volume.**

The purpose of founder-led outreach is to discover a repeatable ICP, repeatable pain, repeatable message and ultimately a repeatable sales process for Chaplin Pro.
