RECENT LESSONS

INTERPRETATION

A strong conversation may not book a demo. A weak conversation may still advance commercially. Review both outcomes without merging them into a single score.

No employee rankings, automatic personality profiling or inferred causal claims.

COMMUNICATION ADAPTABILITY TIPS

Choose the circumstance you observed. These are situational suggestions, not personality categories or prospect labels.

Useful conversation bridges

Opening

“I'm the developer behind Chaplin Pro. May I ask one quick question about how your team coordinates client decisions?”

Discovery

“How does updated client information reach everyone involved?”

Demo request

“In 20 minutes, you can see a real workflow and decide whether it fits your existing process.”

Follow-up

“You suggested I reconnect this week; is there a convenient time for the demonstration?”

CORE PRINCIPLE

Adapt your communication style, not your integrity or claims.

Do not label prospects with fixed personality types. Someone who is abrupt today may simply be busy.

A question often creates more relevance than a long feature explanation.
