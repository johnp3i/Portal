# Portal Sales Module

## Review & Enhancement Recommendations

### Purpose

This document captures recommended enhancements following the review of
the current Sales module implementation.

## 1. Lead Board

Current strengths: - Clear Kanban pipeline - Excellent workflow
visibility - "Today's Actions" drives execution - Product and user
filtering

Recommendations: - Add lead priority (Hot/Warm/Cold) - Display next
required action - Show days since last activity - Show waiting status -
Display proposal expiry where applicable

Every card should answer: **What should I do next?**

## 2. Lead Detail

Replace separated sections with a unified chronological timeline.

Example: Lead Created → Auto acknowledgement → Manual email → Phone call
→ Meeting → Proposal → Won/Lost

## 3. Product Configuration

Extend products with: - Default pipeline - Proposal template - Demo
template - Email templates - Trial duration - Product owner - SLA -
Default onboarding workflow

## 4. Response Templates

Support additional placeholders: - {{Company}} - {{Phone}} -
{{MeetingDate}} - {{MeetingLink}} - {{AssignedSalesperson}} -
{{BusinessWebsite}} - {{ProposalLink}} - {{NextStep}} - {{SupportEmail}}

## 5. Communication Health

Track: - Email delivered - Email opened - Link clicked - Customer
replied - Phone completed - Meeting booked

This quickly highlights communication problems.

## 6. AI Suggested Actions

Future Operational Intelligence examples: - "No response for 4 days.
Call tomorrow morning." - "Proposal viewed multiple times today.
Recommend calling."

AI should recommend, not decide.

## 7. Operational Timeline

Create one activity stream including: - Lead created - Emails - Calls -
Meetings - Proposals - Customer conversion

## 8. Operational Metrics

Track: - New leads - Response SLA - Demo conversion - Proposal
conversion - Win rate - Revenue by product - Revenue by source - Average
sales cycle

## 9. Native Integrations

Integrate Sales with: - Identity - Businesses - Billing -
Subscriptions - Quotations - Invoices - Notifications - Operational
Intelligence

## 10. Strategic Direction

The module is evolving beyond CRM into a Commercial Operations Platform.

Portal Core: - Identity - Businesses - Sales - Billing - Subscriptions -
Customers - Products - Operational Intelligence - Administration

## Guiding Principle

Every new feature should help users make a better decision or complete
the next action. Maintain the clean, workflow-first design language
already established across Portal.
