# Document Numbering Conventions

**Last revised: 12 July 2026**

This document defines the numbering format for all formal business documents generated by the Portal platform.

---

## Invoices

**Format:** `INV-{BusinessId}-{Sequence}`

| Part | Description | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| `INV` | Fixed prefix | INV |
| `{BusinessId}` | The business's internal ID | 1 |
| `{Sequence}` | Zero-padded 5-digit auto-increment per business | 00093 |

**Example:** `INV-1-00093`

**Rules:**
- Sequence is unique per business, never recycled
- Generated atomically at invoice creation via `InvoiceSequence` table
- The BusinessId is included for internal traceability across the multi-tenant database

---

## Credit Notes

**Format:** `CN-{BusinessId}-{Sequence}`

| Part | Description | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| `CN` | Fixed prefix | CN |
| `{BusinessId}` | The business's internal ID | 1 |
| `{Sequence}` | Zero-padded 5-digit auto-increment per business | 00012 |

**Example:** `CN-1-00012`

**Rules:**
- Same pattern as invoices — unique per business, never recycled
- Generated at credit note creation time

---

## Payment Receipts

**Format:** `REC-{InvoiceSeq}-{PaymentNumber}-{DDMMYY}`

| Part | Description | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| `REC` | Fixed prefix | REC |
| `{InvoiceSeq}` | The invoice sequence number (without prefix/BusinessId) | 00093 |
| `{PaymentNumber}` | Which payment this is for that invoice (1st, 2nd, etc.) | 1 |
| `{DDMMYY}` | Receipt date in day-month-year format | 150726 |

**Examples:**
- First payment on invoice 93: `REC-00093-1-150726`
- Second partial payment on invoice 93: `REC-00093-2-180726`
- Third payment: `REC-00093-3-220726`

**Rules:**
- `InvoiceSeq` is extracted from the invoice number (last segment after splitting by `-`)
- `PaymentNumber` counts existing non-voided receipts for that invoice sequence + 1
- For **multi-invoice (global) payments**, the lowest invoice sequence in the group is used
- Voided receipts do not reduce the payment counter — numbers are never recycled
- The date reflects when the receipt was generated (payment date, not creation date)

**Multi-invoice example:**
A global payment covering INV-1-00093 and INV-1-00094 produces:
`REC-00093-1-150726` (uses the lowest sequence: 00093)

---

## Quotations

**Format:** User-defined reference (no enforced pattern)

Quotation references are manually entered by the user (e.g., `Q-2026-001`, `PROP-LeParisRoasting`). There is no system-enforced numbering — businesses use their own conventions.

---

## Design Principles

1. **Customer-facing documents omit internal IDs** — Receipt numbers don't include BusinessId because they're shared with customers who don't need that information.

2. **Traceability** — Every document number can be traced back to its source. Receipt numbers reference the invoice they cover.

3. **No recycling** — Voided documents keep their numbers. The counter always moves forward.

4. **Date inclusion in receipts** — Receipts include the date to provide temporal context without needing to open the document.

5. **Human-readable** — Numbers should be easy to read over the phone or in an email. Short segments separated by hyphens.
